Connection & Security

Encrypted WebRTC peer-to-peer connection. Two-Way QR works without Internet on a reachable local network. CONNECT codes use public PeerJS signaling and require Internet. Campus firewalls or client isolation can block either direct connection.

Refreshing the Web App, restarting Android or switching networks clears pairing and controller permissions. Pair again with a fresh QR. The laptop camera is used only in Step 2.
