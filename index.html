03

USB Serial Monitor

Scanner firmware 1.0.0 has no Wi-Fi, AP or OTA. After USB flashing, open the board's Serial port at 115200 to see its name, firmware version, SDA/SCL pins, detected I²C addresses and scan count. Tap RESET to replay the banner. Arduino IDE Serial Monitor works too.

This is a standalone diagnostic sketch; it does not drive motors. A1 default GPIO8/9 are boot strap pins (GPIO9 is BOOT); A2 uses XIAO D4/D5.
